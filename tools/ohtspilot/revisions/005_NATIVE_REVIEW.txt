LEPRO offroad native comparison completed, 2026-11-25

Scope: installed controller 003 versus candidate 005, using actual Planner,
native acados MPC and LongControl. Each of 10 scenarios has 320 planner steps
per variant (6400 solves); LongControl is called 5 times per step at 100 Hz.
Vehicle acceleration follows the command instantaneously in this ideal plant.
Navigation/curve solutions and vision vectors are substituted. This is neither
recorded-route replay nor vehicle validation. No CAN messages are sent.
Installed source hashes remained unchanged; candidate is not deployed.

All solves returned status zero and finite measured results. In these short,
sequential offroad runs, planner median was about 7.3-7.6 ms. This does not
establish onroad timing or explain the prior communication fault.

Key results:
- Tight launch: initial 4 m gap, hold released and lead accelerates at t=2 s.
  Both variants exceed 0.1 m/s at t=2.8 s. No launch-time improvement.
- Fast launch: initial 8 m gap, hold released and lead accelerates at t=2 s.
  Both exceed 0.1 m/s at t=2.05 s. Candidate speed at t=10 s is only
  0.05645 km/h higher (9.21426 versus 9.19857 m/s).
- Hold, stationary lead and close approach have identical trajectories.
- Restop minimum gap: 3.83804 m baseline, 3.83749 m candidate.
- Hard-braking minimum gap: 3.94787 m baseline, 3.94731 m candidate.
- Close approach minimum gap: 3.61350 m in both variants.
These below-4 m results must not be reported as passing a 4 m hard clearance
criterion. The planner's stop-distance preference is not a guaranteed bound.

The generic launch case permits creeping before the lead moves because its
initial gap is 8 m with no hold. Its launch_time is NOT a lead-response delay.
Use the explicit tight/fast held-start cases for the response comparison.

Interpretation: removing the low-speed exclusion and raising its planning
ceiling did not materially improve launch in these cases. The existing
LongControl starting state still commands CP.startAccel (2 m/s^2 observed),
independently of the candidate ceiling. In other states the optimizer can
choose acceleration below the raised ceiling. Further ceiling increases are
not supported by these results. No road-test readiness conclusion is made.

Next evidence needed: recorded launch alignment of lead motion, hold/state
transition, planned acceleration, commanded acceleration and vehicle response;
also identify the service causing communication alerts. Retain the installed
003 controller plus 004 diagnostics while those issues are unresolved.
