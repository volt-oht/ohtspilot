Revision 005: low-speed departure candidate, not installed on LEPRO

Request: include standstill and speeds <= 2 m/s in the catch-up allowance.
Simply removing the speed gate would leave no allowance at zero speed:
the original base ceiling and the previous absolute ceiling are both 1.4.

Changes:
- Accept nonnegative speed through 80 km/h, including zero.
- Accept the existing PID and starting states. Stopping and off remain excluded.
- Use cruiseState.standstill to preserve the vehicle's hold condition; a zero
  wheel speed alone no longer vetoes an already-authorized departure.
- For v < 2 m/s, ceiling = max(1.4, base + 0.3 * (1 - v / 2)).
  The final limit remains min(ceiling, base + 0.3 * lead_strength).
  At zero speed, 1.7 m/s^2 is an experimental planning ceiling, not a forced
  command or a validated safe vehicle acceleration. At/above 2 m/s, the
  original 1.4 ceiling is retained. The transition is continuous.

Preserved conditions: healthy radar; moving, nondecelerating radar lead;
relative speed > 0.5 m/s; distance surplus > 2 m above stop distance plus
time headway; both leads can veto; no pedals or forceDecel; no dynamic cost
or stop-line mode; measured and planned acceleration nonnegative; cruise
target above current speed; Volt with openpilot longitudinal control only.
Turn limits remain in place. No change to stop hold, state transitions,
brake release, startAccel, longitudinal PID, CAN messages or safety checks.
In particular, this cannot force a departure while the controller is stopping.

Validation: 11 helper tests and 6 actual Planner.update input tests passed.
Tests cover low-speed/zero-speed ceiling changes, boundary continuity,
stationary/closing/braking/close leads, missing radar, both pedals, hold,
stopping state and decelerating plan. Planner tests use a recording MPC;
they do not execute the optimizer or simulate the vehicle.

Native comparison subsequently completed on LEPRO; see 005_NATIVE_REVIEW.txt.
Not validated: actual launch comfort, vehicle brake-release timing, onroad
CPU load or communication fault cause.
No LEPRO deployment or onroad test performed for this revision. Keep the
installed 003 controller plus 004 diagnostics until those checks are complete.
The original kanspilot repository is unchanged.
